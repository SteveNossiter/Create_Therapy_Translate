Information & Security

Quick Start Instructions

  1. Prepare: Enter the client's initials and select your desired report template.
  2. Record: Tap the Record stone. Your device screen will automatically lock awake.
  3. Process: Tap Stop, then Generate. Keep this window open and active until your notes appear.
  4. Clear Workspace: After exporting your notes, tap 'Clear Session' to securely hide the record in your Archive.

Security Architecture Update

  • Server-Side Encryption at Rest: Notes are encrypted securely inside Cloud Run prior to being stored in Supabase, preventing database exposure risks.
  • Crash Recovery: Session audio is silently cached to your device's IndexedDB while recording. If your browser crashes or reloads mid-session, your audio is preserved.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): The database physically blocks access to any records that do not belong to your specific authenticated user token.
