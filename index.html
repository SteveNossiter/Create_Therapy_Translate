Quick Start Instructions

  1. Prepare: Enter the client's initials. Your defaults are pre-filled.
  2. Record: Tap "Start New Session" and complete the consent declaration. The device screen will lock awake. A safety cutoff automatically stops recording at 2 hours.
  3. Process: Tap "Finish Session & Secure Notes." The app will quickly upload the file to the cloud.
  4. Clear Workspace: Once the green success screen appears, you can immediately clear the screen for your next client. Notes will process remotely and appear in the Archive.

Security, Privacy & Compliance

  • Data Residency & APP 8: To fulfill cross-border privacy requirements, raw audio is transcribed temporarily via a strict GDPR-compliant data center in Dublin, Ireland.
  • Onshore Clinical Analysis: All semantic reasoning, transcript analysis, and report generation is processed strictly on onshore Google Vertex AI servers located in Sydney, Australia.
  • HRIPA (NSW) Alignment: To comply with Health Privacy Principle 5 (Security), notes are subjected to server-side encryption via Cloud Run prior to storage.
